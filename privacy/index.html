Subscriber information

Privacy, in plain English.

Florida Signal asks for your email address so we can deliver the newsletter you requested. We do not sell your personal information.

What we collect

When you subscribe, we collect your email address, ZIP code and the campaign/source of the link you used (if present in the URL). Our email provider may also report delivery, opens and link activity so we can understand whether the briefing is useful.

How we use it

We use subscriber information to deliver Florida Signal emails, maintain the list, prevent abuse and improve the briefing. Access is limited to the Florida Signal Desk and service providers that operate the newsletter.

Privacy-minimized analytics

We use first-party analytics to record pages viewed and whether a signup succeeds. A random session ID is stored in your browser’s session storage for the current session. Analytics do not receive your email, ZIP code, exact location or anything you type into a search field.

Your control

Every newsletter includes an unsubscribe link. You may also ask to review, correct or remove your subscriber information by emailing desk@thefloridasignal.com.

Effective August 16, 2026. We will date material changes to this notice.
