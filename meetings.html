What “watching” means. Florida Signal checks the official government calendar, preserves the meeting body, date, time and location, and links out to the source. Industry rows come only from an association’s official event page and are never labeled hearings.

What comes next. The duplicated Michigan Intel Desk supplies the donor pattern for agenda extraction, source validation, relevance review, stale-meeting expiry and newsletter nomination. Florida-specific collectors can plug into this page without changing the public contract.
