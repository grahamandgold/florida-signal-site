## Lead Signal · reporting candidate

### Central City land-use and redevelopment funding moves onto Tuesday's agenda

**State: needs direct memo and attachment review before this can be reader copy.**

The Fort Lauderdale Community Redevelopment Agency Board has two Central City items scheduled for
Tuesday, Aug. 18 at 1 p.m. One would amend the city/CRA interlocal agreement to authorize funding for
projects that implement the Central City Redevelopment Plan. The other would authorize up to
$189,952 for MHCP COLAB, LLC to prepare a Future Land Use Plan amendment for the area. A parallel
interlocal item is also listed for the 6 p.m. City Commission meeting.

- What changed: current agenda packets now place the funding and land-use work before public bodies.
- Why it may matter: this is an earlier project-policy signal than a building permit, but the actual
  development effect cannot be stated until the scope, funding path and conditions are read.
- Proof to open: CRA Board items R-1 and R-2; City Commission item R-3; staff memos, interlocal
  agreements and the LUPA scope of services.
- Unknown: sites or projects funded, boundary effects, approval conditions, opposition/support and
  later hearing or state-review steps.
- What happens next: report the attachments, confirm the action requested and follow Tuesday's vote.
