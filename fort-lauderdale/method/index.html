How to read every number

A count travels with its clock.

Window
The first and last application, registration, recording or meeting date included.
Sample
The displayed record cap plus the actual date span inside that returned sample.
Snapshot
When a cached source was last successfully pulled or enriched.
Cumulative
A total through a named date—not a claim that every record happened that day.

01 · Collect

Named public sources.

The public site reads a deliberately narrow set of fields from the production Supabase mirror. The mirror is populated from government records; it is not a synthetic dataset.

  • Fort Lauderdale AccelaPermits, applications, status, declared value, contractors, inspections and workflow.
  • Broward BCPAParcel, assessed value, sale history, owner and flood-zone context.
  • Florida SunbizPublic company records used to resolve entities behind filings.
  • Broward ClerkDeeds, mortgages, liens, notices and other recorded instruments.
  • City GISOfficial neighborhood boundaries and parcel-derived coordinates.
  • Legistar + City meeting calendarUpcoming hearings, development meetings, agendas and official broadcast destinations.
  • RWorld + CASFOfficial professional-event listings, separately labeled as industry rooms rather than public hearings.
  • NOAA / NHCCurrent Atlantic storm status and official seven-day tropical outlook.

02 · Join

One address, several records.

Permit number, parcel/folio, normalized address, company name and recording identifiers connect the layers. Confidence and provenance matter: a resolved owner is stored with its source and a confidence field, and low-confidence joins do not get promoted as fact.

The map displays only records with usable coordinates. Neighborhood counts describe the current displayed sample and are never presented as a complete historical total.

03 · Gate

A signal is not automatically a story.

The Michigan Intel Desk donor architecture contributes the important rule: collection, analysis, editorial approval and publication are separate states. Raw signals can appear as sourced records. Narrative claims require evidence and human review before publication.

  • Source gateNo source, no claim.
  • Claims checkMaterial facts must map to a record or document.
  • Managing editorAnalysis, headlines and context do not publish autonomously.
  • Sponsor firewallUnderwriters may sponsor a module or edition; they cannot influence records, rankings, lead qualification, stories or corrections.
  • Correction standardCorrections are visible, dated and linked to the affected item.

04 · Refresh

Freshness is part of the fact.

Every connected data page exposes an independent source-health strip. The browser asks Supabase for the current public record set when a page opens, but a fresh mirror does not make an older Broward or aggregate cache current. Each source is labeled current, delayed, stale, unverified or unavailable from its own clock.

Application, registration, recording and meeting dates describe the market. Pull, first-seen, sync and enrichment times describe the system. If a job misses its expected window, Florida Signal preserves the older date instead of inventing freshness.
