02 The creative direction

A white editorial field, interrupted only by evidence.

Navy is rationed to three places in the whole issue: the masthead, the evidence card, and Place Lens. That restraint is the entire idea — when a reader hits a dark block, they are looking at a record. Everything else is white space, a serif headline and a thin rule. No cards, no shadows, no gradients, no boxed sections.

03 Emblem system

The emblem appears exactly three times in a full issue — masthead, one section transition, footer. That scarcity is what makes it read as a seal rather than a logo. Every placement uses the approved production export; the wordmark is never re-typeset in HTML.

Masthead · centrepiece
Stacked reverse lockup, 340px wide. The only place it appears at scale. Masthead height stays under 210px so the first finding clears the mobile fold.
Section transition · 20px
Navy mono at 50% between a hairline rule. Maximum twice per issue — before The Proof, and before Place Lens. It marks a change of register, not every section.
Watermark · 5% opacity
Watermark · optional
Reserved for The Receipt on the pale field. Never behind body copy, evidence or a map. If it costs any legibility, drop it — most issues will not use it.
Footer · compact cut
The approved no-tagline cut at 200px. Quiet and institutional — it closes the issue, it does not re-sell it.

04 The ten modules

Two modules always ship. Everything else rotates on the strength of the week's journalism. A normal edition runs five to seven modules; a quiet one runs four and still feels complete. Never fill a slot to fill a slot.

Always
THE OPEN
Unconditional. Every edition opens with intelligence, never a greeting.
Only when earned
THE PROOF
The preferred flagship, not a requirement. It runs whenever one Signal clearly earns flagship treatment. If none does, the strongest verified rotator takes the lead position and the edition runs shorter. Never manufacture a flagship to satisfy the template.
Always
THE RECEIPT
Unconditional. The trust contract closes every edition, never shortened.
Universal editorial anatomy

Every substantive module answers the same five questions in the same order. Specialised labels sit on top of this logic — they never replace it.

WHAT CHANGED → THE PROOF → WHY IT MATTERS → THE IMPACT → WHAT'S NEXT + WHAT WE DON'T KNOW

Status language

Each state differs by shape, not colour — solid, outlined, double-ruled, dashed banner. They stay distinguishable in dark mode, in plain text and to a colour-blind reader. No emoji.

ADVANCE
Solid fill. Documented movement forward.
WATCH
Outline. Verified, but the outcome is unresolved.
STALL
Double rule. Documented setback or expiry.
CANDIDATE · NOT YET A SIGNAL
Dashed banner wrapping the whole module — structurally different from a pill, so it can never be mistaken for a verified state.

08 Module population guide
Module Use when Do not use when Items / words Visuals
THE OPEN Every edition, without exception. Never skipped. Never opened with a greeting or an essay. 3–5 items · 20–35 words each None
THE PROOF One Signal clearly earns flagship treatment. Preferred, never required. Nothing clears the threshold — the strongest rotator leads instead and the edition runs shorter. 1 item · 250–400 words Rendering, map, crop or chart
SIGNAL BOARD Two to four further Signals cleared verification. Only one Signal exists, or items would repeat the lead. 2–4 items · 60–90 words each Rarely — keep it typographic
THE MONEY Deeds, mortgages, liens or assemblage show a documented pattern. A single ordinary sale with no pattern behind it. 1 item · 150–250 words Parcel map, record table
THE ROOM An agenda item could shape development before permits. The meeting is routine or the item is procedural. 1–2 items · 120–200 words Agenda excerpt, site plan
THE OPERATORS An operator changed how they are moving through the system. Only raw volume — a ranking is not a Signal. 1 subject · 120–180 words Two metrics, optional chart
ON THE RADAR Something is consequential but unverified. It already cleared verification — then it belongs above. 1–2 items · 100–150 words Doc crop or map
STALL WATCH A record documents withdrawal, expiry, denial or deferral. Only silence — absence of filing is not a setback. 1–2 items · 80–140 words Rarely
PLACE LENS Several records converge on one geography. A single site — that is The Proof, not a lens. 1 geography · 180–280 words Largest map in the edition
THE RECEIPT Every edition, without exception. Never skipped, never shortened, never restyled. Fixed · sources vary Optional watermark

Word counts are ceilings, not targets. Every module can be deleted whole from the master between its START MODULE / END MODULE comments without touching anything else.

Built for Monday, not locked to it.
The day appears in exactly one place — the masthead metadata bar — and nowhere in the brand, the copy or the module names. Going to five days a week means changing one string per edition. A daily cadence simply runs shorter configurations: The Open + one rotator + The Receipt on a light day, the full lead on a heavy one. Nothing in the system needs redesigning to get there.
