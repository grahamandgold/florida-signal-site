Three rules for every visual
Alt text is mandatory. Describe what the image proves, not what it is. Outlook blocks images by default, so the alt line is what most readers see first.
Caption states the takeaway. Never describe the picture — say what the reader should conclude from it. The credit follows in the lighter tone on the same line.
Nothing critical in a background. Every image is a real <img> with width and alt. No CSS background images, which Outlook drops silently.
