Dek2442 E. Sunrise Blvd. · Galleria / East Sunrise Boulevard

What Changed / Why It Matters / What We Don't Know — verbatim
What ChangedThe trail starts June 26 with interior-demolition, electrical and plumbing filings at unit Z17. A sprinkler-demolition filing followed July 15. On Aug. 4, a $556,991 main permit described landlord work to prepare the empty retail space, including selective structural reinforcement; five trade applications followed Aug. 11. The filings cover overlapping work, so their declared values should not be added together. Open the $557K main permit →
Why It MattersThe locator shows Aventura Mall in Miami-Dade as the nearest listed South Florida location. The long-vacant anchor sits inside the Galleria's mall-wide mixed-use repositioning. Wayfair has separately announced a 2027 store. Neither announcement names Zara. The permits establish what was filed with the city, not what is signed.
What We Don't KnowWhether a lease is signed; the proposed store size, format or opening timetable. BCPA measures the entire building at 96,748 square feet; that is not a Zara-space figure. As of Aug. 17, Florida Signal found no public Zara, Inditex or Galleria announcement naming Zara at this address. We also found no indexed prior report.

Broward Clerk records show 106 Fort Lauderdale deeds recorded Aug. 3–9 with nearly $205 million in stated consideration, the amount reported on the deeds rather than an appraisal.
